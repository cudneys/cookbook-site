Gluten Free Cranberry Bread with Orange, White Chocolate, and Hazelnuts

Moist gluten free cranberry bread with orange, white chocolate, and hazelnuts is the ultimate holiday quick bread! Dairy free option.

Prep 20 min
Cook 1 hr 15 min
Total 2 hr 15 min
Serves 1
Source Flippindelicious

Instructions

  1. 1
    Preheat the oven to 350F. Lightly grease and dust a 9x4 inch loaf pan with gluten-free flour (you can use a regular loaf pan, but will have to adjust the baking time.)
  2. 2
    Whisk together the gluten-free flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl and set aside.
  3. 3
    In a stand mixer, or in a bowl with heavy-duty electric beaters, cream together the butter and sugar until fluffy.
  4. 4
    Add the eggs one at a time, beating until smooth after each egg. Add the vanilla and orange zest.
  5. 5
    Add the flour mixture and mix until smooth.
  6. 6
    Add the orange juice and buttermilk and mix until combined.
  7. 7
    Fold in the cranberries, chocolate chips, and hazelnuts.
  8. 8
    Pour the batter into the prepared 9x4 loaf pan and bake for approx 75 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted near the center of the loaf comes out clean OR the bread springs back when lightly touched.
