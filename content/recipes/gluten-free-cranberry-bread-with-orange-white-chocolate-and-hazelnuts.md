  "In a stand mixer, or in a bowl with heavy-duty electric beaters, cream together the butter and sugar until fluffy.",
  "Add the eggs one at a time, beating until smooth after each egg. Add the vanilla and orange zest.",
  "Add the flour mixture and mix until smooth.",
  "Add the orange juice and buttermilk and mix until combined.",
